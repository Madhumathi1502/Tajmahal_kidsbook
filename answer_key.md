## PAGE 8 — THE DOME CHALLENGE

**Activity: Stable or Unstable?**

| Structure | Answer | Reason |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| A — Wide base, centred dome, large arch | STABLE | Wide base distributes weight; centred dome balanced |
| B — Narrow base, dome off-centre | UNSTABLE | Narrow base insufficient; off-centre dome creates imbalance |
| C — Wide base, thin walls, dome on arches | STABLE | Arches redirect weight; wide base distributes load |
| D — Base narrower at bottom than top | UNSTABLE | Inverted taper = top-heavy; structurally impossible |

**Thinking Question:** Open-ended. Expected: a wide, stable base; thick enough walls; good structural support; even weight distribution.

**Label note:** Activity clearly labelled "MODERN ACTIVITY INSPIRED BY HISTORICAL ARCHITECTURE." This is not a reconstruction of Taj Mahal building methods.

## PAGE 20 — THE INSCRIPTION INVESTIGATION

**Activity: Investigate the Inscriptions**

Expected answers:

1. **Placement:** Inscriptions are placed at the top of arches and around the borders of gateway and mausoleum portals — in prominent visible positions.
2. **Border observation:** Borders are decorative frames — geometric or floral — that separate the inscription text from the surrounding wall surface.
3. **Scale relative to human figure:** The letters are very large — several times the height of a person — making them readable from a distance.
4. **Open-ended reflection:** Expected reasoning: a monument inscription is permanent and public, meant to be seen by everyone who visits for centuries. A book inscription is private and temporary by comparison. The placement and scale suggest the builders wanted these words to be prominent and enduring.

**Historical accuracy:** Optical size correction confirmed in Section 7 of fact_sheet.md. No symbolic claim about specific verse meanings is made in the activity.
