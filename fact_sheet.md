## SECTION 4 — THE ARCHITECTURAL COMPLEX

The Taj Mahal is a complex of multiple buildings and spaces. It is not only the central white marble mausoleum.

### Confirmed Components

| Component | Description | Primary Material | Confidence |
|-----------|-------------|----------|-----------|
| Mausoleum (tomb) | Central building; contains the cenotaphs and tombs of Mumtaz Mahal and Shah Jahan | White marble | ✅ HIGH |
| Central dome | Large bulbous dome crowning the mausoleum | White marble | ✅ HIGH |
| Four minarets | Slender towers at each corner of the mausoleum platform | White marble | ✅ HIGH |
| Platform (chabutra) | Raised terrace on which the mausoleum and minarets stand | White marble | ✅ HIGH |
| Mosque (Masjid) | On the western side of the mausoleum | Red sandstone | ✅ HIGH |
| Jawab (Guest house / mirror building) | Eastern side — mirrors the mosque; used as assembly hall or guest pavilion | Red sandstone | ✅ HIGH |
| Darwaza-i Rauza (Great Gate / Main Gateway) | Main entrance gateway at the south | Red sandstone | ✅ HIGH |
| Charbagh garden | Four-part symmetrical garden between gateway and mausoleum | Planted | ✅ HIGH |
| Central water tank (hauz) | Raised square tank at midpoint of garden; reflects the mausoleum | Stone | ✅ HIGH |
| Garden water channels | Two main intersecting channels dividing the garden into four quadrants | Stone | ✅ HIGH |
| Outer courtyard and cloisters | Forecourt and surrounding structures | Red sandstone | ✅ HIGH |

> **BOOK NOTE:** The minarets' exact historical purpose is not definitively documented in sources reviewed. Do NOT claim a specific purpose. Describe their visual effect only.

## CRITICAL REMINDERS FOR MANUSCRIPT WRITING

1. Never name a specific architect as definitive fact
2. Always say "approximately 20,000" — never an exact figure
3. 1653 refers to the outlying structures only — not "the Taj Mahal was finished in 1653"
4. Do not present the black marble legend
5. Do not present the hand-cutting myth
6. Do not invent symbolic meanings for architectural features
7. The reflecting pool is a garden feature — do not assign invented symbolic meaning
8. The minarets' purpose is not definitively documented — do not invent one
9. The charbagh can be described as drawn from garden traditions associated with paradise — use cautious, historically accurate wording
10. Always distinguish: mausoleum = the central building; Taj Mahal complex = everything including gardens, gateway, mosque, jawab, outer courts
