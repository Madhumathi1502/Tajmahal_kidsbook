## 2. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

### Fonts to Use (System/Google Fonts — print safe)

| Role | Font | Weight | Size (at 300dpi) | Notes |
|------|------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| **Book Title** | Cinzel Decorative | Bold | 72–84pt | Page 1 only |
| **Page Header / Chapter Title** | Cinzel | SemiBold | 28–36pt | Every page top |
| **Section Header (H2)** | Cinzel | Regular | 20–24pt | Activity titles, vocabulary spotlights |
| **Subheader (H3)** | Lato | Bold | 16–18pt | Table headers, sub-sections |
| **Body Text** | Lato | Regular | 12–13pt | All educational content, story |
| **Activity Instructions** | Lato | Bold | 13–14pt | Stand out from body |
| **Writing Lines / Labels** | Lato | Regular | 11–12pt | Child's fill-in areas |
| **Captions / Fiction Labels** | Lato | Italic | 9–10pt | Illustration captions, badges |
| **Page Numbers** | Cinzel | Regular | 10pt | Bottom centre |
| **Vocabulary Words** | Cinzel | SemiBold | 14pt | Vocabulary spotlight boxes |

### Typography Rules

- **Line spacing:** 1.4× for body text; 1.2× for headers
- **Paragraph spacing:** 8pt after each body paragraph
- **All headings:** Deep Indigo (`#2C3E6B`) or Sandstone Red (`#B5533C`)
- **Body text:** Charcoal Ink (`#2B2B2B`) — never pure black on ivory
- **Fiction labels:** Always italic + badge style (rounded rectangle background)
- **Vocabulary words:** Always in a framed box with Parchment Beige background
- **NO ALL-CAPS body text** (headings only)
- **Minimum readable body text size on printed page:** 11pt

## 6. ILLUSTRATION STYLE GUIDE

### Overall Aesthetic
The illustration style should feel like a **premium children's educational book** — detailed, warm, and historically evocative, without being overly cartoonish or overly realistic.

**Reference feeling:** Explorer's journal meets museum activity book.

### Illustration Style Specifications
- **Rendering:** Digital painting with visible brushwork — not flat vector, not photorealistic
- **Line style:** Soft ink outlines, slightly imperfect (hand-drawn quality), 2–3px
- **Colour application:** Warm, layered colour washes; not flat fills
- **Texture:** Paper/parchment texture overlay on all illustrations (10–15% opacity)
- **Lighting:** Warm ambient light; golden hour feel for outdoor Mughal scenes
- **Atmosphere:** Historical, evocative, slightly mysterious — never threatening

### What Illustrations Must Show
- ✅ Historically accurate 17th-century environments where applicable
- ✅ Correct Taj Mahal architecture: **exactly 1 central dome, exactly 4 minarets, correct proportions**
- ✅ Red sandstone for mosque/jawab/gateway; white marble for mausoleum
- ✅ Age-appropriate, warm, and engaging character designs
- ✅ HISTORICAL RECREATION label on all period-scene illustrations

### What Illustrations Must NOT Show
- ❌ Modern buildings, cars, electricity poles, phones, modern clothing
- ❌ 5 minarets or 3 minarets — always exactly 4
- ❌ Extra domes, distorted arches, impossible geometry
- ❌ European castle features, Chinese pagoda features, fantasy towers
- ❌ Named historical individuals shown as realistic portraits claiming authenticity
- ❌ Legible AI-generated text inside illustrations (garbled text, fake writing)
- ❌ Specific individual faces on unnamed historical workers
- ❌ Modern equipment or machinery in historical scenes

### Illustration Check Protocol (per image generated)
Before accepting any illustration:
1. Count the minarets (must be 4)
2. Count the domes (must be 1 central dome on mausoleum)
3. Check for modern elements
4. Check for garbled text
5. Check character consistency (explorer character only)
6. Check historical costume accuracy

## 7. MAIN EXPLORER CHARACTER

### Character Concept
**Name in book:** "The Explorer" (child can name them on Page 1)  
**Type:** Fictional child explorer — NOT a historical person  
**Age:** Approximately 10–11 years (within the 8–12 target range)  
**Gender presentation:** Deliberately non-specific/neutral — child can project their own identity  

### Visual Design

| Feature | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| **Hair** | Dark brown, short-medium length, slightly wavy; visible under hat |
| **Skin tone** | Warm medium-brown — reflects the South Asian setting |
| **Build** | Average child proportions; slightly tall for age |
| **Eyes** | Dark brown, wide and curious; large in illustration style |
| **Expression** | Default: curious and engaged; varies by scene (focused, surprised, excited) |

### Clothing (CONSISTENT across all pages)

| Item | Description | Colour |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **Hat** | Round-brimmed explorer's hat, slightly worn | Warm tan/khaki |
| **Shirt** | Simple collared shirt with rolled-up sleeves | Cream/off-white |

| **Trousers** | Simple loose trousers, slightly baggy | Stone grey-brown |
| **Shoes** | Simple leather boots/sandals | Brown leather |

### Accessories (CONSISTENT)

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Explorer's Portfolio** | Worn leather satchel/bag with brass clasp; carried at all times when in story scenes |
| **Magnifying glass** | Brass-handled, round lens; often held or clipped to belt |
| **Notebook** | Small, tucked into vest pocket; visible but not always open |

### Character Poses (Reference Set)
The explorer should appear in these specific poses across the book:

| Page | Pose | Scene |
|------|------|-------|

## 12. CONSISTENT DESIGN RULES — DO/DON'T CHECKLIST

### ALWAYS DO
- ✅ Apply page border frame to every page
- ✅ Place page number in footer, centred
- ✅ Use book title in footer, left-aligned
- ✅ Label every fictional element with the appropriate badge
- ✅ Label every historical recreation illustration
- ✅ Keep all body text outside AI-generated illustrations
- ✅ Apply parchment texture overlay to all illustrated sections
- ✅ Use only palette colours — no spontaneous new colours
- ✅ Use only specified fonts — no random font changes
- ✅ Check Taj Mahal architecture: 1 dome, 4 minarets, on every relevant page
- ✅ Keep explorer character visually consistent

### NEVER DO
- ❌ Place critical text inside AI-generated image regions
- ❌ Use neon, pure RGB red/green/blue, or random colours
- ❌ Generate Arabic, Persian, or Quranic text in illustrations
- ❌ Show named historical individuals claiming authenticity
- ❌ Include modern objects in historical scenes
- ❌ Change the explorer character's design between pages
- ❌ Deviate from 8.5 × 11 in portrait, 300 DPI
- ❌ Allow text to run outside the safe margin zone
- ❌ Cover activity writing spaces with illustrations
- ❌ Show Taj Mahal with wrong number of minarets or domes
